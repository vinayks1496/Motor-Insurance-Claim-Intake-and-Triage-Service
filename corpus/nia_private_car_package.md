# Private car package policy: indexed evidence notes

This file is a compact offline fallback for the official wording listed above. When the source PDF is available, the indexing command reads its full text and replaces these notes with page-level chunks.

## NIA-PC-OD-COVER

Section I covers loss or damage to the insured vehicle and attached accessories from listed events. The list includes accidental external means, fire, theft, malicious acts, specified natural events, and transit. This is a possible own-damage grant only; all other policy terms and exceptions still apply.

Source excerpt: “loss or damage to the vehicle insured” (Section I, page 1).

## NIA-PC-GENERAL-EXCEPTION-USE

General Exception 3 limits cover where the vehicle is being used outside the schedule's permitted use or is driven by someone who does not meet the driver clause. The exception can modify an apparent Section I cover.

Source excerpt: “otherwise than in accordance with the Limitations as to Use” (General Exceptions, page 3).

## NIA-PC-CLAIM-NOTICE

Condition 1 asks the insured to notify the company in writing immediately after accidental loss or damage and to provide information and assistance requested. Theft or a criminal act should also be reported to police immediately.

Source excerpt: “Notice shall be given in writing” (Conditions, page 3).
