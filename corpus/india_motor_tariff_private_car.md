# Private car use restrictions

This supplementary source is hosted by New India Assurance and sets out the private-car scope and schedule wording. It must be read alongside the policy's own-damage grant and General Exception 3.

## IMT-PC-SCOPE-001

Tariff section 2 describes private cars as being used for social, domestic, pleasure and professional purposes, with carriage of goods other than samples excluded. It also excludes use for hire or reward, racing, pace making, reliability trials, speed testing, and motor-trade purposes.

Source excerpt: “excluding the carriage of goods other than samples” (Tariff section 2, page 26).

## IMT-PC-USE-001

The private-car schedule's permitted-use clause excludes hire or reward; carrying goods except samples or personal luggage; organized racing; pace making; speed testing; reliability trials; and motor-trade use.

Source excerpt: “Hire or Reward” (Limitations as to Use, page 26).

## IMT-PC-DRIVER-001

The driver clause requires an effective driving licence and no current disqualification. A learner's licence may be accepted only when the stated rule requirements are met.

Source excerpt: “holds an effective driving license” (Driver clause, page 26).
