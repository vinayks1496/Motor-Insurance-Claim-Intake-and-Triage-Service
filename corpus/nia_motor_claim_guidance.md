# Motor claim procedure guidance

## NIA-CLAIM-ACCIDENT-INTIMATION

For an accident, the guidance says to inform the nearest or policy-issuing office, follow with written intimation, and submit the completed claim form and a repair estimate. It calls for a spot survey for commercial vehicles, major accidents, or accidents involving multiple vehicles.

Source excerpt: “completed claim form & estimate of repairs” (Do's and Don'ts, page 1).

## NIA-CLAIM-THIRD-PARTY

For third-party death, injury, or property damage, notify police immediately and inform the policy-issuing office with the incident and driver details.

Source excerpt: “inform the police immediately” (Do's and Don'ts, page 1).

## NIA-CLAIM-THEFT

For vehicle theft, notify police and the office on the same day and lodge an FIR immediately.

Source excerpt: “Lodge an FIR immediately” (Do's and Don'ts, page 2).
